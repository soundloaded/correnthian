## 2. Strategic Analysis: The 6 Core Questions

### 2.1 Where are competitors strong?
1. **BullionVault & Paxos on Verifiable Proof**: BullionVault's Daily Audit and Paxos's Ethereum-address-to-bar-serial lookup tool don't ask customers for blind faith — they provide verifiable mechanisms.
2. **OneGold on Jurisdictional Clarity**: Naming the exact vault operators (Brink's, Loomis, Royal Canadian Mint) and countries (Switzerland, UK, Canada, US) immediately answers the gold buyer's first question: *"Where is the metal physically sitting?"*
3. **Goldmoney on Executive Tone**: Goldmoney's copy (*"You own the metal. We look after it. Clear costs. Considered decisions."*) speaks to capital preservationists as adults rather than speculators.
4. **Trust Wallet on Key Sovereignty**: Clearly stating *"We don't control or have access to your private keys — only you do"* sets an unmistakable security boundary.

### 2.2 Where are competitors weak, confusing, or overly complex?
1. **The Custodial Trap (OneGold, BullionVault, Goldmoney, Kinesis Platform)**: Traditional digital gold platforms require the customer to keep their balance inside a closed platform database. You cannot hold your gold in your own cryptographic wallet alongside ETH or USDC, and you cannot transfer it 24/7 on-chain without platform approval.
2. **The "Blind Wallet" Problem (Trust Wallet, MetaMask)**: General-purpose crypto wallets treat a $100,000 position in `PAXG` or `XAUT` the exact same way they treat a valueless ERC-20 token. They show no fine troy ounce / gram weight, no custodian, no vault location, no proof-of-reserve link, and no distinction between allocated physical backing and a synthetic derivative.
3. **Yield & Tokenomics Overload (Kinesis Money)**: By bundling gold with 6 different fee-sharing yields, KVT velocity tokens, Currency One stablecoins, and "up to 12% APY" promotions, Kinesis triggers skepticism among conservative bullion buyers who simply want unencumbered ownership of metal.
4. **Multi-Layer Wrapper Opacity (KMS Labs / Wrapped Metals)**: Retail buyers rarely realize when an on-chain token is a secondary wrapper (e.g., KMS Labs S.A. in Panama holding Kinesis Cayman tokens) rather than a direct claim on the primary bullion issuer.

### 2.3 What questions would a careful gold, silver, or crypto buyer still have after visiting their websites?
A skeptical buyer moving $25,000–$250,000 into digital metals asks five questions that existing sites rarely answer in one place:
1. **"Do I actually hold the token in my own wallet, or is it just an IOU in your company's database?"**
2. **"Who legally issues the gold or silver token, is it allocated to specific physical bars, and what happens if your app goes out of business tomorrow?"**
3. **"How much of my purchase is being eaten by hidden bid/ask spread Markup versus explicit fees?"**
4. **"Can I really take physical delivery of a gold bar, or is the minimum 400 troy ounces (~$1M+)?"**
5. **"What happens if I lose my phone — do I lose my life savings because of a 12-word paper seed phrase?"**

### 2.4 What lessons should Correnthian borrow?
- **From Goldmoney**: Short, declarative, unhurried headlines and a restrained 5-item top navigation.
- **From BullionVault**: Upfront fee itemisation and unvarnished honesty about physical bar redemption minimums versus on-chain liquidity.
- **From Paxos & KMS Labs**: Explicit legal-chain diagrams showing the exact relationship between **Your Wallet -> The On-Chain Token -> The Regulated Issuer -> The Physical Vault & Auditor**.
- **From OneGold**: Prominent vault-location and custodian metadata on every precious-metal view.
- **From Trust Wallet**: Unambiguous explanation of non-custodial key control, upgraded with Correnthian's passkey-first `SmartAccount` architecture.

### 2.5 What should Correnthian explicitly avoid copying?
1. **Never copy yield, APY, staking, or "earn on gold" promotions** (violates Correnthian's compliance boundary and alienates serious bullion buyers).
2. **Never imply Correnthian is the token issuer or vault operator** (Correnthian curates and verifies third-party tokens via `packages/assets`; this independence is our superpower because we have no conflict of interest).
3. **Never copy Kinesis's mega-menu sprawl** (25+ sub-products, merchandise stores, gold bills, velocity tokens).
4. **Never copy neon purple/cyan "Web3 degen" aesthetics** or cartoon mascots.

### 2.6 How can Correnthian feel clearer, more modern, and more trustworthy than all of them?
By making **separation of powers and radical disclosure** the centerpiece of the user experience:
- **You control the keys** (Passkey Smart Account + optional Seed export; Correnthian's servers are mathematically and CI-enforced to hold zero keys).
- **Independent issuers vault the bars** (Every token displays its exact `BackingDisclosure`: `allocated` vs `unallocated` vs `synthetic`, issuer legal entity, Brink's/Swiss vault location, and latest auditor attestation link).
- **Every fee is named before you sign** (Provider fee, network gas, and Correnthian fee itemised on three separate lines — zero hidden spread).
